# Crater Crane

A one-button lunar freight shift. Hit Space, click or tap the yard, or press gamepad A to lower the magnet. Choose a cyan crate for one load anywhere on the rover deck, or an amber diamond for two loads in its narrower center socket. Once it is attached, release it ahead of the passing rover. The crane sweeps faster after each delivery. Four deliveries clear the shift and three misses end it. P or Escape pauses.

Orbit Pond and Solar Kite build an arcade around a familiar activity in an unfamiliar place. Here the player works a freight crane under a ringed planet. The magnet, cell and rover are visible throughout the run. The home card is a native-resolution capture of the title scene. The game uses Solar Kite’s bitmap font and edge treatment.

Run the root site check to play a complete shift through the arcade card and verify the retired Signal Bloom bookmark.

Reduced motion keeps the hoist and drop trajectories visible and uses a stationary score label in place of sparks, trails and the impact wave. The aurora, refinery and terrain are painted into a cached backdrop.
