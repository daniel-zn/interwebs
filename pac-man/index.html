Arrow keys or W A S D steer Pac-Man. On a touch screen, swipe in the direction to go. Eat every dot to clear the maze. Energizers turn the ghosts blue for a few seconds so you can eat them. Enter, Space or a tap starts a game. P pauses, M toggles sound, H opens help and settings.

Paused

The ghosts will wait. They're patient like that.

How to play

  1. Steer Pac-Man round the maze and eat every dot to clear the level. You can press a direction early and he turns at the next gap.
  2. Touch a ghost and you lose a life. The side tunnel wraps round, and ghosts slow down inside it.
  3. The four big energizers turn the ghosts blue. Eat them for 200, 400, 800 and 1600 points before they flash white and recover.
  4. Bonus fruit appears under the ghost house twice a level. You get an extra life at 10,000 points.
  • Blinky chases you directly, and speeds up when few dots are left.
  • Pinky aims four tiles ahead of you to cut you off.
  • Inky works with Blinky, so he's hard to predict.
  • Clyde chases until he gets close, then loses his nerve.
