              How to play

              Power turns sound on. Keys 1–6 play the chapters, Q–I the hits. Space starts the loop, Esc stops everything. Each chapter has its own instrument: carillon bells for UBC, a sonar ping for the fish-monitoring startup, a warm grid pad for the utilities commission, wood for student teaching, a bright lead for the science classroom, and brass for the airport sales floor.
