Eight impossible websites

Eight working prototypes, each a different answer to what a personal website can be. They all use the same content: 27 projects, a résumé and 20 sets of course notes. Each one opens full screen and works with a mouse, keyboard or touch.

    Sound plays only after you press something. Every concept has a plain list of the projects, so the content is never locked behind the gimmick.
