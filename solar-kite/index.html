How to fly a solar kite

  1. The solar wind blows away from the sun. The kite always flies forward, so you only steer it. Hold the mouse or a finger and the kite turns towards it, or spin it with ← →.
  2. It is fastest in the middle of the wind window and slows to a hover at the dotted edge. Don't fly it into the ground.
  3. Tricks: turn a full circle for a loop, then circle the other way for a figure 8. Go round a floating object to orbit it (without hitting it), thread flux rings, and skim low over the dust.
  4. Tricks chain into a combo. Every different trick adds 1 to the multiplier (up to x10), so mix them up: repeats score less and barely add to it. The combo banks when you stop for a moment. A crash or a bonk loses it.
  5. Loops twist the lines. Too many twists make the kite sluggish, so loop back the other way to untwist (that's a trick too).
  • * Solar flare: stronger wind, and every trick scores double.
  • ~ Lull: the wind drops and the kite sags. Stay low and central, and tug.
  • ! Radio: the kite club asks for a trick. Pull it off in time for a bonus.
  • o Flux rings: fly through all of them for a ring chain.
