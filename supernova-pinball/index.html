Z or the left arrow flips the left flipper, slash or the right arrow flips the right one. Hold Space to pull the plunger and let go to launch. Up arrow nudges the table; nudge too much and it tilts. On a touch screen, touch the left or right half of the screen to flip, hold to pull the plunger, and swipe up to nudge. P pauses, M toggles sound, H opens help and settings. Big moments are read out as they happen.

How to play

  1. Each sector is a star system with a score target. Reach it and the ball warps to the next one, and you pick one of three upgrades. Run out of balls and it's over. Clearing a sector gives you a ball back.
  2. Hit the pop bumpers, slingshots, both drop target banks, the S T A R lanes (flip to move the lit lanes), the spinning orbits and the ramps. The small flippers up top shoot the ramps.
  3. Light all four lanes to raise the playfield multiplier (up to ×5). Knock down a bank of targets for a big bonus, or roll over all nine S U P E R N O V A letters.
  4. Everything you hit feeds the dying star in the middle. When its mass meter is full it goes SUPERNOVA: extra balls and double scoring for 20 seconds.
  5. New things arrive as you go deeper: wormhole locks and multiball (sector 2, hit the star for jackpots), comets (3), a roaming black hole (4) and an alien mothership (5).
  6. Nudge the table to save a ball, but nudge too hard and it tilts: dead flippers until the ball drains.
