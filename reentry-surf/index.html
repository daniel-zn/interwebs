How to surf re-entry

  1. Hold to dive, let go to lift. Pump between the two to carve a smooth line.
  2. Ride the band between the glowing hot line below and the dashed thin line above. Below it the HEAT gauge fills. Above it the SKIP gauge fills. If either one fills up, the run is over.
  3. Skim close to either edge for style points. Clean riding builds your flow multiplier up to x5.
  4. Slow down enough and your chute opens. Ride it all the way to a splashdown.
  • ! Space junk: dodge it. A hit adds heat and resets your flow.
  • ^ Updraft: lifts you. Watch out when you're already high.
  • * Storm band: the hot air bulges up into a big wave. Ride over the crest for a bonus.
  • > Jet stream: ride the ribbon to cool down and score fast.
  • + Coolant: grab it to vent heat.
