Use one button: Space, Enter, a mouse click, a touch, or gamepad A. Hold to charge a cast and release to throw. Longer casts reach rarer fish. Wait for a bite, then press right away. Hold to reel the fish in, and let go when the line strains. Press L for the star log, M to mute.

Star Log

    Settings

    How to play

    • Hold Space / Enter / click / touch to charge a cast, release to throw. Farther casts reach stranger fish.
    • Watch the bobber. Nibbles are teases; when it plunges with a “!”, press right away.
    • Hold to reel. When the line strains or the fish pulls, let go for a moment.
    • Every fish goes back. Where it swims off to, a new star appears.
    • Keys: L star log · M mute · Esc close. Gamepad A works too.
