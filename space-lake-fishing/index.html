One button game. Hold Space, Enter, the mouse button or your finger to charge a cast, and let go to cast. When the bobber plunges, press once to hook the fish. Then hold to reel and let go whenever the line strains. Press J for the fish journal, M to toggle sound, H for help and settings.

Fish journal

    How to fish

    1. Hold to wind up, let go to cast. The meter swings back and forth — farther casts reach rarer fish.
    2. Wait. Small twitches are nibbles. When the bobber plunges and a “!” pops up, press once to hook it.
    3. Hold to reel. When the line meter strains or its frame flashes orange, let go until it calms. Fill the lower bar to land your catch.
