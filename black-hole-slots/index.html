Space, Enter, the lever or the spin button spins the reels. At the pit stop and in menus, arrow keys move between choices and Enter picks one; you can also click or tap. M toggles sound, H opens help and settings. Results are read out as they land.

How to play

  1. Your slot machine is drifting into a black hole, and the hole wants coins. Every round there's a debt due in three days. Pay it or fall in.
  2. Each day starts at the pit stop: spend tickets on charms, then pick a deal: lots of spins, or fewer spins and more tickets.
  3. Pull the lever. Matching symbols in a row, column, diagonal or shape pay the symbol's value times the pattern's mult. All matches on screen pay.
  4. Three or more Void Eyes anywhere and the void eats a quarter of your coins. They get more common every round.
  5. Pay a debt and the void sends a transmission: pick one permanent gift. Pay all eight debts to escape, then keep going in endless mode if you dare.
